COMBUSTION is a chemical reaction where a substance reacts rapidly with oxygen and releases energy into the surroundings, primarily as thermal energy (heat) and light.

The difference between complete combustion and incomplete combustion?
Complete combustion occurs when the fuel is fully burned and make carbon dioxide. Incomplete combustion happen when the fuels in not completely burned and made carbon monoxide as product.

WORD EQ
COMPLETE COMBUSTION: fuels + Oxygen (O2) -> carbon dioxide + water + heat
INCOMPLETE COMBUSTION: fuels + Oxygen (O2) -> Carbon monoxide + water + heat


CHEMICAL EQ
COMPLETE COMBUSTION: CyHxOz + O2 -> CO2 + H2O + heat -> fuel is fully burned
INCOMPLETE COMBUSTION: CyHxOz + O2 -> CO + H2O + heat -> when the fuel is not completely burned

How combustion occur in engine and how it trigger the movement of the wheels?
Energy from a spark starts the combustion reaction between the hydrocarbon fuel (CxHy0z) and oxygen from the air. Combustion is an exothermic reaction, so it releases thermal energy.
The combustion produces hot gases , including carbon dioxide (C02), carbon monoxide (CO) and water vapor(H20). The hot gases expand, creating pressure that pushes the piston down. The movement of the piston provides kinetic energy, which is transferred through the engine to turn the wheels and move the car.
The waste gases are then pushed out through the exhaust.

What is Exothermic and endothermic reaction?
Exothermic reactions release thermal energy into their surroundings, 
while Endothermic reactions absorb thermal energy from their surroundings

For combustion to occur, three specific factors must be present at the same time:
• Fuel: Typically a hydrocarbon (like methane, petrol, or propane).
• Oxidizing Agent: Oxygen (O₂), usually supplied from the surrounding air.
• Ignition Source: Thermal energy (heat) to overcome the activation energy barrier.

Why combustion always exothermic ?
Combustion is always classified as an exothermic reaction because it transfers chemical energy from the reactants to the surroundings as thermal energy.

Why is carbon monoxide (CO), a product of incomplete combustion, particularly hazardous to human health?
It binds irreversibly to hemoglobin in red blood cells, reducing oxygen transport.
Carbon monoxide binds tightly to hemoglobin, preventing red blood cells from carrying oxygen efficiently around the body, which can be fatal.

22. Why do we need to use specific things to put out fire?

To reduce or extinguish a fire, we need to eliminate one or more factors of the fire triangle: oxygen, heat, and fuel.

Why we don't use water for burning oil?
Water removes heat, but when water is added to burning oil, it can rapidly turn into steam and cause the burning oil to spread, making the fire spread further.
For an oil fire, we use foam or a fire blanket to block the supply of oxygen, stopping combustion.

Short:
Because water will sinks through the oil and make steam, spreading the fire further. To extinguish the fire we need to stop the combustion fully by eliminated the oxygen using fire blanket or foam.

Why we don't use water for electrical fire ?
For an electrical fire, we use a powder or carbon dioxide extinguisher. Water should not be used because water conducts electricity, creating an electrical hazard. Carbon dioxide helps extinguish the fire by displacing oxygen around the flame, so combustion cannot continue.
